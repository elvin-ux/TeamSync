APP NAME

TeamSync

TAGLINE

Collaborate. Assign. Track. Deliver.

PROBLEM

Small and medium teams often manage projects through a mix of WhatsApp groups,
emails, spreadsheets, and meetings.

This causes:

- Missed deadlines
- Lack of task ownership
- Poor project visibility
- Communication gaps
- Difficulty tracking productivity

TeamSync centralizes project management, task tracking,
team collaboration, and reporting into a single platform.

Persona 1: Project Manager

Persona 3: Team Member

Needs:
